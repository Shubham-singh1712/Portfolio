AutoSpend AI

Automating Personal Expense Management

AI-powered expense management platform that extracts transactions from receipts, screenshots, and text inputs.

View Source Live Demo

Problem Statement

Manual expense tracking is tedious. Users often forget to log transactions, and categorizing them takes time. Existing OCR solutions are rigid and fail on messy receipts.

Solution

AutoSpend AI uses advanced LLMs combined with OCR to intelligently parse unstructured financial data. Whether it's a screenshot of a payment app or a crumpled receipt, it extracts amount, date, vendor, and category automatically.

Key Features

  • ✦ Smart Receipt Scanning & OCR
  • ✦ Natural Language Expense Logging
  • ✦ Automated Categorization Dashboard
  • ✦ AI-Assisted Review Workflows
